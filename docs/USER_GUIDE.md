# User Guide & Feature Manual — StudentOS

Welcome to **StudentOS: An AI-Powered Digital Twin for Personalized Student Success**. This guide walks through each major module and feature of the application.

---

## 1. Onboarding & Account Creation
1. Navigate to `/register` and enter your name, email, and password.
2. Complete the **5-Step Onboarding Wizard**:
   - **Step 1: Academic Profile:** College, degree, branch, current year/semester, and baseline CGPA.
   - **Step 2: Enrolled Subjects:** Add courses, credits, and initial attendance status.
   - **Step 3: Goals & Habits:** Target CGPA and daily study hour preferences.
   - **Step 4: Career & Programming:** Primary goal (e.g. AI/ML Engineer) and preferred language.
   - **Step 5: Review & Initialize:** Builds the initial Digital Twin baseline.

---

## 2. Daily Operations & Tracking Modules

### Academic Curriculum (`/academic`)
- View all active courses with credit weightings.
- Add assessment marks (mid-terms, quizzes, labs) to keep your internal assessment average up to date.
- Review automatic performance status tags: **Strong**, **Moderate**, or **Needs Attention**.

### Attendance Tracker & Calculator (`/attendance`)
- Log classes conducted and attended for each subject.
- Subjects falling below the 75% threshold are marked with **Critical Warning** badges.
- Use the **Target Attendance Calculator** to determine exactly how many consecutive lectures you must attend to achieve 75% or 80%.

### Assignment Hub (`/assignments`)
- Track upcoming deliverables, estimated completion hours, and deadlines.
- View your **7-Day Workload Distribution** to avoid deadline clustering.

### Study Tracker & Focus Diary (`/study-tracker`)
- Log daily self-study hours, focus rating (1-5), and subject time breakdowns.
- Monitor your **14-day study consistency score**.

### Coding Practice & DSA Tracker (`/coding`)
- Log algorithmic problem attempts across LeetCode, HackerRank, and Codeforces.
- Track current streaks, difficulty distributions (Easy/Medium/Hard), and set quantifiable milestones.

---

## 3. Intelligence & Simulation Modules

### Student Digital Twin (`/digital-twin`)
- Visualizes your complete 4-dimensional virtual state: Academic, Behavioral, Skill, and Coding.
- Shows your **Predicted Next Semester CGPA**, **Risk Level**, and **Data Completeness Gauge**.
- Review the **Derived Feature Vector** and historical snapshot progression.

### What-If Simulation Sandbox (`/simulator`)
1. View your current baseline values on the parameter sliders.
2. Enter a scenario title (e.g. "+1.5 Hours Study + 90% Attendance").
3. Drag the sliders to test prospective habit changes.
4. Click **"Run Counterfactual Simulation"**.
5. Observe the side-by-side comparison:
   - **Projected CGPA Delta** (e.g. $+0.38\text{ CGPA}$)
   - **Risk Shift** (e.g. Moderate $\to$ Low)
   - **Key Influencing Drivers**
6. Click **"Save Scenario"** to bookmark the strategy for future reference.

### AI Recommendations Hub (`/recommendations`)
- Displays prioritized interventions generated by deterministic rule heuristics.
- Filter by status (Active, Accepted, Completed) or category (Academic, Attendance, Study, Skills, Coding).
- Accept recommendations or mark them completed as you execute them.

### Intelligent Study Planner (`/planner`)
- Click **"Generate New Plan"**, choose date range and daily hours.
- The planner algorithmically balances sessions, prioritizing weak subjects and low internal marks.
- Check off sessions daily on the interactive calendar.

### Advisory Assistant (`/assistant`)
- Converse with the AI advisor grounded in your live database records.
- Click starter prompts or suggested quick-action chips to explore interventions.
